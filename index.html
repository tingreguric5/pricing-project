FAQ

Which offer is right for me?
VoltPoint Pro is for specialists who install many chargers: next-day stock, training, hotline, subsidy paperwork and 60 days to pay. VoltPoint Direct is for occasional installers: a simple online order, paid in advance, with courier delivery and the same 48-hour warranty swap.
How is my Pro price set?
Your price per charger depends on how many chargers you bought in the previous year: €600 for up to 49, €585 for 50–119 and €570 for 120 or more. Every installer pays the published price for their band – there are no negotiated discounts.
Can I get help with the subsidy?
Yes, with VoltPoint Pro. The national subsidy can be filed only by certified installers and only for chargers bought in Croatia; we prepare the paperwork for you.
Do I have to sell at €949?
No. €949 including VAT is our recommended consumer price. You set your own price.
